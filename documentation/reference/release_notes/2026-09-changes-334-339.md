# Model changes of 24-25 September 2026 (#332, #334-#339)

Plain-language record of changes that can move your results. If your numbers
change after updating and you have not edited your own data, look here first.
The technical record (with the exact pinned figures) is
`../GOLDEN_MASTER_CHANGELOG.md`.

## Changes that can move your results

| Change | What you may notice | Inputs affected | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| **#334 Medicare IRMAA indexing and corrected 2025 table** | IRMAA surcharge thresholds now grow with inflation (CPI) from the 2025 table, rounded the way Medicare rounds them, and the Part B/Part D surcharge dollars now grow with medical inflation. Before, thresholds grew at a fixed 2% a year. The 2025 starting table itself was also corrected to the published CMS figures (for example the married-filing-jointly tier 2 threshold is $266,000, not $268,000). The old "IRMAA annual inflator" input no longer exists. | Filing status, income in the two years before Medicare (the IRMAA lookback), medical and CPI inflation assumptions | Mixed. Higher-inflation plans see thresholds rise faster, so fewer years are surcharged; the corrected lower married-filing-jointly thresholds pull some years the other way. In the frozen sample plan the net effect was small: terminal net worth moved by about +$2,500 on roughly $5.3M and lifetime tax by about -$500 on roughly $1.28M. Roth-conversion and withdrawal-cap choices that key off the IRMAA threshold can shift by a year or two. |
| **#336 Large Discretionary is one-time** | Large Discretionary items (big trips, weddings and similar) are now single dated entries and are never spread across years. An older item entered with a start and end year is converted on load into one one-time row per year, with a notice. Total spending should be unchanged after conversion, but an item that used to be annualized by a category budget now appears only in the years listed. | Large Discretionary rows, category or group budgets with no year | None expected from the conversion itself; lower spending in any year you had not dated. |
| **#338 Next Housing Move switch controls housing plan inputs** | If **Next Housing Move** is turned off on Plan Features, the projection now ignores the saved home sale, next-housing-step and state-residency values (they are kept and listed on the page, and applied again when you turn it back on). Before, those values applied whatever the switch said. | Home sale year and splits, Next Housing Step 1-2 fields, state residency schedule | If you had entered a sale, a move or a state change and the switch was off, the plan now assumes you stay in your current home and state, which changes spending, taxes and net worth. |

## Changes that do not move results

- **#335 Spending Adjustments** is new. Nothing changes until you add an adjustment (a percent step-down or step-up on a spending category from a chosen year).
- **#337** The "Annualized" label is now used consistently across the Spending Model. Labels only.
- **#338 layout** Housing, Wellness and Travel edit in the Spending Model; home value and mortgage balance moved to Other Assets and Liabilities; the Housing and Wellness nav steps redirect there. Same numbers.
- **#339** The unused "Checking accounts" field on Reserve Requirements was removed. The projection never read it (cash comes from holdings in a Checking account), so results are unchanged.
- **#332** Plan Features categories, left navigation groups and workbook sections now share one topic vocabulary; workbook sections gain a Topic column. Sheet order can change within a section; sheet contents do not.

## How to tell a model change from a change in your data

Rebuild with the same plan file before and after updating. If Plan Data has not
changed and the results have, the difference comes from a row above.
