Net worth

Net worth = investments (live holdings) + annuities/pension + home, startup equity, cash and autos from the plan inputs, minus the mortgage (amortized from its plan-start balance) and any other liabilities. Home and other plan values are held at their plan-start figures, so day-to-day movement is mostly investments.

Liquid Investments and Cash

Investment net flows by account (YTD)

Year-to-date transfers between each investment account and checking, as of the latest snapshot. A net withdrawal (money moved to checking) is positive and extends right; net contributions, including 401k employee deferrals and employer match, are negative and extend left. Money moved from one investment account to another through checking is not counted. A withdrawal and a same-amount deposit in different investment accounts within 30 days are matched automatically; pairs within $100 of each other are flagged above for you to approve or deny; and any transaction tagged "invest-to-invest" (or noted "pass-through") is excluded.
