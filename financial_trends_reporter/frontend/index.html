Financial trends

YTD expenses by category, holdings value/performance, net worth, and cashflow — one entry per weekday close. Scroll a chart sideways to see earlier dates.

Net worth

Net worth = investments (live holdings) + annuities/pension + home, startup equity, cash and autos from the plan inputs, minus the mortgage (amortized from its plan-start balance) and any other liabilities. Home and other plan values are held at their plan-start figures, so day-to-day movement is mostly investments.

Cashflow (income vs. expenses)

Cashflow on any date = year-to-date income minus year-to-date expenses, from Jan 1 through that date (a running total, not a single day's or month's flow). Expenses include income and real-estate taxes paid, so this figure is lower than the category total shown in the expenses chart.
