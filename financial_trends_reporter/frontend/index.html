Financial Trends

Four charts: Net Worth, Liquid Investments and Cash, Investment Net Flows, and Spending — one entry per weekday close. Net Worth and Liquid Investments and Cash use daily Imported account balances. The Time scope pulldown sets the dates every chart covers. The data updates automatically when this page opens.

Net Worth

Net worth = investments (live holdings) + annuities/pension + home, startup equity, cash and autos from the plan inputs, minus the mortgage (amortized from its plan-start balance) and any other liabilities. Home and other plan values are held at their plan-start figures, so day-to-day movement is mostly investments.

Investment Net Flows

Year-to-date transfers between each investment account and checking, as of the latest snapshot. A net withdrawal (money moved to checking) is positive and extends right; net contributions, including 401k employee deferrals and employer match, are negative and extend left. Money moved from one investment account to another through checking is not counted. A withdrawal and a same-amount deposit in different investment accounts within 30 days are matched automatically; pairs within $100 of each other are flagged above for you to approve or deny; and any transaction tagged "invest-to-invest" (or noted "pass-through") is excluded. Decisions apply right away: the page updates the data after you approve or deny.
